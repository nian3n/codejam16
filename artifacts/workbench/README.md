# Workbench captures

Chrome at 1440 × 900. The camera in the active capture replays a demonstration from the PopSign ASL v1.0 dataset. The browser test uses controlled landmark inputs so the same round can be reproduced. [PopSign ASL v1.0](https://signdata.cc.gatech.edu/view/datasets/popsign_v1_0/)

The backdrop was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and saved as `src/web/static/workshop.jpg`. The case, controls, and damage are rendered in the browser.

## Backdrop prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create original background art for a browser puzzle game. A dim industrial electronics repair workshop, environmental shot, landscape 3:2. Stationary camera facing a broad, completely empty earthy charcoal-brown wooden workbench from a mildly elevated angle, nearly straight-on so the bench and back wall have horizontal lines. The desk surface spans the entire width in the lower half, to the bottom edge, with subtle wood grain, scuffs, a muted warm pool of light from a shielded tungsten task lamp in the upper LEFT. Do not draw anything on the table, it is the live gameplay compositing surface. Rear wall is dark charcoal teal painted cinderblock, with a narrow amber utility lamp hanging from the top left, old black vertical pipes to the edges and a vague equipment rack far right in deep shadow. Moody, cinematic and tactile, a believable low-key 3D render, warm left light and faint cool teal reflected light on the right. Keep the middle simple, table broad and uninterrupted, foreground reading as natural wood. Soft vignette at edges. Original art. No device, case, wires, wreckage, lettering, interface, logos, signage, people, or chairs.
